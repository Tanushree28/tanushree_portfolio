About Me

I study where AI systems fail — not what they achieve under ideal conditions, but how they break down under noise, conflicting evidence, or incomplete information. My work spans biomedical RAG stress-testing (thesis, 3 accepted papers) and deep reinforcement learning for sequential decision-making under uncertainty, unified by one question: how do we know when a model can be trusted, and when it shouldn't be. Open to PhD research and applied ML/data science roles.

Aug 2026 – Present · Advisor: Dr. I Hua Tsai · MIT-BIH Arrhythmia (PhysioNet)

Fairness vs. Accuracy in Machine Learning Models

May 2026 – Present · Advisor: Dr. Gorkem Kar

Built a PyTorch training method with a tunable accuracy–fairness trade-off, evaluated across five real-world decision-making datasets (criminal risk, income, credit default, health coverage). Reduced demographic-group disparities by up to 93% at under 0.5% accuracy loss.
